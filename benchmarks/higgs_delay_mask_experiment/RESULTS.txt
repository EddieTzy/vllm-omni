Change:

existing CPU delay/remaining counters in request order, transfer one small
int32 table, and apply the existing BOS/EOS allowed-token rules in one

logit values. No weights, public APIs, deployment defaults or dependencies
change. Other GPU platforms, graph modes and multi-GPU were not qualified.

Definitions and interpretation:
Audio throughput is actual audio seconds/full measured client wall time.
RTF is the arithmetic mean of each request's E2E/audio duration, not inverse
aggregate throughput. E2E includes HTTP, server queueing, talker and codec;
TTFA means first nonempty PCM bytes, near completion in this deployment.
Unseeded outputs differ in length; both candidate boots and their spread are
reported. Two boots are descriptive evidence, not a confidence interval.
No general perceptual/speaker-quality, multi-GPU, CUDA-graph or hardware-wide
speed guarantee. No new full ASR/SIM sweep or full repository CI was run.
The controlled waveform equality applies to its stated32-prompt workload.
Do not add this percentage to the earlier B200 routing/state-fusion results.
