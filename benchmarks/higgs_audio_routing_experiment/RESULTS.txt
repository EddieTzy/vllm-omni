Higgs v2 uniform-audio routing

Status: submitted upstream as PR #11068.
https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni/pull/11068
This perf branch freezes the measured source and its evidence; the upstream
PR branch was rebased separately. No second optimization is included here.

Change:
Resolve the all-audio mask once per forward and reuse the existing direct
audio norm/MLP path across 28 decoder layers. The baseline already skips
absent text FFN rows; this removes repeated mask reductions and boolean
indexing, not previously executed text FFN compute. Text/BOS/mixed paths
retain tensor masks. One scalar synchronization remains per forward.
Three production lines plus regression coverage; no API, weight, sampling,
deployment-default or dependency changes, and no CUDA-graph support claim.

Measured baseline: 4dafb464442956996be9633ed036cd24ebfef1d0.
Measured routing implementation: 30dcfdc205d17072734328e74c5ca96a882ec548.
Exact model-source hashes and public model/data revisions: manifest.json.
The rebased PR source has the same talker hash.

Hardware: one NVIDIA B200, 183359 MiB, driver 580.173.02.
Software: torch 2.14.0+cu130, vLLM 0.34.0, FlashInfer 0.7.3,
transformers 5.22.0, vLLM-Omni measured baseline above.
Both arms: BF16, TP=PP=1, no quantization; two-stage MRv2 eager deployment,
async_chunk=false, max_num_seqs=32 in both stages, GPU fractions .6/.25,
prefix caching and async scheduling disabled. Stage max_model_len=4096/65536,
max_num_batched_tokens=2048/65536. Audio sampling temperature=1, top_k=50,
top_p=.95, max_tokens=1024; no speech-request seed.

Serving:
Two independent boots per variant, chronological A/B/A2/B2.
Public SeedTTS targets fixed before measurement: 32 distinct EN targets of
6-20 words and 32 distinct ZH targets of 10-36 characters. No voice cloning.
Native C16: per-completion replacement, 16 warmups +32 measured per language.
Raw C32: streaming PCM client, 32 warmups +64 measured in two waves of 32,
interleaved EN/ZH. These protocols differ and are reported separately.
No additional concurrency levels were benchmarked for this archive.

All measured requests succeeded. RTF is mean(request latency/audio duration),
not inverse aggregate throughput. Latency includes HTTP, queueing and both
stages. First audio arrives near completion in this tested deployment.

Configured pools are included; no memory reduction claim.

Correctness and validation:
Existing Higgs MRv2 regression module: 16 passed, 17 warnings. The two new
all-audio cases fail on the original implementation; text/BOS controls pass.
All applicable pre-commit hooks passed for the two PR files, including mypy.
Actual BF16 norm/MLP shadowing: 32424 strict comparisons across 28 layers
and 386 all-audio forwards, all bitwise equal.
Controlled real-model C32 A/B/A2 with common canonical admission and initial
audio RNG: 32/32 complete PCM, 32/32 code histories and all 179 normalized
sampling/RNG events equal. Diagnostic overhead is not performance evidence.
Detailed PCM hashes: controlled_output_equivalence.json.

Quality interpretation:
All 256 unseeded raw outputs were ASR-scored, zero exclusions. Original
unpaired pooled EN WER: baseline 12/712, routing 11/712. Original unpaired
ZH CER: baseline 7/1176, routing 13/1176. Preserve these raw observations;
they compare different stochastic samples and are not a paired regression
test. Fixed-order repeated ASR retained the same normalized rates. Frozen
logits/RNG replay demonstrated request-row/random-draw coupling. Under the
controlled matched batch/RNG test, all outputs were byte-identical and CER
was 24/588 in all three arms, including the high-error utterance. That
different controlled workload is not an absolute quality improvement.
The original boots did not record full RNG trajectories; their exact sample
histories cannot be reconstructed. No general perceptual/speaker-quality
noninferiority, deterministic-seed API, TP/multi-GPU or full-CI claim is made.
